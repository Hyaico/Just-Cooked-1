# Account & Data Deletion Policy

_Last updated: 11 June 2026_

This page explains how to delete your **Just Cooked™** account, what we delete, what we are required to keep, and how long it takes. It forms part of our Legal Documents alongside the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use**.

---

## 1. How to delete your account

**In the app:**

1. Open Just Cooked
2. Go to **Profile → Settings**
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm deletion

**By email:** contact [support@justcooked.app](mailto:support@justcooked.app) from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may need to verify your identity first.

---

## 2. What happens straight away

- You are signed out and your account can no longer be used.
- Your data stops being used for any personalisation or analytics.
- The deletion process begins.

---

## 3. What we delete

Within **30 days** of your request, we delete or anonymise the personal data linked to your account, including:

- Your profile (name, email address, username, profile photo)
- Your onboarding answers, including anything you told us about your relationship with food
- Allergy and dietary information
- Body details and any calorie or protein targets
- Support and wellbeing data, including your gentle mode and display settings and any note you wrote in the Panic tool
- Emergency contacts, including their names, numbers and photos
- Your pantry, saved recipes, folders and notes
- Your cooking history, logged nutrition and cooking photos
- Recipes you shared via public links (the links stop working)
- Your app settings and preferences
- Your analytics profile (we also instruct our analytics provider to delete person-level data linked to your account)

---

## 4. What we keep, and why

Some records must or may lawfully be retained after deletion:

- **Purchase and transaction records**, kept for up to **6 years** to meet UK tax and accounting obligations
- **Security and audit logs**, anonymised or deleted within **12 months**
- **Aggregated or anonymised statistics** that can no longer identify you
- Information we reasonably need to **establish, exercise or defend legal claims** or to **prevent fraud and abuse**, for as long as necessary for that purpose

---

## 5. Backups

Your data is also removed from our encrypted backups as they cycle out. Backup copies are fully purged within **90 days** of deletion. Backups are not used to restore deleted accounts.

---

## 6. Subscriptions are separate

**Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.** Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us, and must be cancelled in your store account:

- **iPhone / iPad:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Just Cooked → Cancel
- **Android:** Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Just Cooked → Cancel

If you delete your account without cancelling, the store will continue to bill you until you cancel there.

---

## 7. Want a copy of your data first?

Email [support@justcooked.app](mailto:support@justcooked.app) **before** deleting your account and we will provide a copy of your personal data, as described in our Privacy Policy. Deletion is permanent and we cannot recover your data afterwards.
